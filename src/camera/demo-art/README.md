# Demo camera art

Hand-painted versions of the two demo cameras (`street`, `carport`), made for the น้องฝน art package
(the app's earlier vector frames restyled so the ground keeps its place). `src/camera/demo-scene.js` draws the water, rain and camera stamp on top.

`*-occluders.png` mark what stands in front of the water (gate posts, gate, plant; the car, the raised step, the
side wall). Water is cut out wherever they are opaque. Rebuild them if a picture changes.
